* Overview

This document records the v0.7 action plan developed from a full critique of
v0.6 (all 48 pages). The critique identified a core pattern: the body text is
honest about open derivations; the abstract and section headers are not. The
mismatch is the primary thing to fix. No new physics is required for Phase 1.

* Title Change

Current: /The Medium: A Foundational Theory of Reality/

Proposed: *The Medium: A Foundational Framework for Physics*

Rationale: "Theory" implies derivational completeness the document does not yet
have. "Framework" is accurate. "of Reality" signals to physicists that you are
outside their conversation. "for Physics" positions the work as an alternative
foundation for the physics that already works — which is exactly the claim.

** What you can do

Develop the mode structure *requirements* from what the framework must produce.
This is conceptual, not technical: what properties must the relational modes have
to generate three fermion generations, half-integer closure, and the gauge
structure the Standard Model observes? Working backward from the endpoint
constrains the mode structure without deriving it. That work is yours to do.

** What requires collaboration

- Formalizing the combination rules from a derived mode structure
- Deriving the particle table from combination rules
- Showing the projection boundary account produces the observer's dimensional
  categories
- Completing the LQG, string theory, and Planck bit derivations

** The single blocking dependency

ι mode structure. Everything else is downstream of it. Until the relational mode
structure is characterized, the adjacency constraint is working-version only, the
particle table derivation is provisional, and the algebraic hierarchy cannot be
revised from scalar summation to the correct combination operation.

This should be stated at the top of Unresolved Questions, not distributed across
entries.

** Format suggestion

Convert Unresolved Questions from a list of gaps into a research roadmap with
three tiers:
1. *Blocking* — must be resolved for downstream work (mode structure, adjacency
   reformulation)
2. *Dependent* — open until blocker resolves (combination rules, particle table,
   ℏ derivation, Lorentz invariance)
3. *Independent* — can proceed in parallel (BBN alternative account, CMB
   blackbody mechanism, Higgs placement, Pauli exclusion from adjacency)
