\section{Definition}
\label{sec:org4685efa}

The Medium is a pre-geometric substrate with no beginning and no end. Its sole primitive is \(\iota\): an act of self-distinction. Each \(\iota\) event produces a binary character (\(+1\) or \(-1\)) --- the difference the distinction makes. Character is not held by \(\iota\) before the event; it is produced by the event.

\(\iota\) has multiple relational modes (qualitatively distinct aspects of the event, not binary opposition) and intrinsic cohesive attraction. From these two properties, self-organization follows without instruction.

The substrate field records distinction characters across the relational web:

\begin{equation}
\phi : M \to \{+1, -1\}
\end{equation}

Two \(\iota\) events of identical character cannot be directly co-manifested (adjacency constraint). This is a logical necessity, not an imposed rule.

\section{Dynamics}
\label{sec:org9eb0cb3}

The negotiation field \(N_{ij}\) encodes the boundary state between adjacent \(\iota\) events \(i\) and \(j\). Lagrangian in Planck units:

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(\frac{\partial N}{\partial t}\right)^{\!2} - \tfrac{1}{2}\,N^\top L\, N
\end{equation}

Hamiltonian (via Legendre transform, \(\Pi = \partial N/\partial t\)):

\begin{equation}
\hat{H} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Pi^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}\,N^\top L\, N
\end{equation}

Energy eigenvalues: \(E_k = \varepsilon\,\lambda_k\), where \(\lambda_k\) are eigenvalues of the graph Laplacian \(L\).

Stable particles satisfy the closure condition \(L \cdot N = 0\). For a complete graph on \(n\) nodes with mode number \(k\), energy eigenvalues follow:

\begin{equation}
E(n,k) = \varepsilon \cdot k \cdot n
\end{equation}

The minimum closed graph is the three-\(\iota\) triangle (\(\lambda = 3\), \(n = 3\)):

\begin{equation}
m_\text{proton} = 3\varepsilon = 938.3\ \text{MeV}
\end{equation}

Higher topologies follow the same formula: a two-node graph corresponds to the rho meson (\(n=2\), predicted 625.6 MeV); a four-node graph corresponds to the tetraquark (\(n=4\), predicted 1251 MeV). The triangle is the ground state --- the minimum topology that achieves closure.
