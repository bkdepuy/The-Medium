* Who I Am

I retired from coding nearly 20 years ago and now have the time to explore questions plaguing me for some time.

I have wondered, what if I had pursued an academic path in Physics and Mathematics.  Probably both a blessing and a curse.
It would be a curse due to the constraint to prove my self within the tightly closed box of institutional acceptance.
This would have limited my ability to see other possibilities.
It would have been a blessing as I may now see the mathematical patterns necessary to expand the documents that follow.

That is worth saying at the start, because it matters for how you read what follows. The
five documents in this series are my attempt at a new foundational approach.

This document is different. This one is about the question
--- where does it all come from.

* What I Kept Coming Back To


The question "why something rather than nothing" assumes that nothing is the natural state
and something requires an explanation. But what if that's backwards? What if nothing is the
harder thing to sustain? What if, given an infinite domain with no constraints and no
preferred states, the emergence of /something/ is not a puzzle requiring explanation but an
inevitability?

I couldn't prove that. But it kept feeling more honest than the alternatives.

The other thing I kept coming back to was the strangeness of how much the universe
apparently cares about mathematics. The equations of physics work. Not approximately ---
with extraordinary precision. The ratio of the proton mass to the Planck energy, the
cosmological constant, the fine structure constant --- these numbers are what they are, and
when you write them down together, they form patterns that feel like they're trying to say
something.

And then there was the incompatibility problem. Quantum mechanics and general relativity
are both right. They are also mutually incompatible. Physicists have known this for a
century. My suspicion, sitting with this for a long time, was that they are incompatible
not because we haven't found the right math yet, but because they are both descriptions of
the same thing from inside the thing, and descriptions from inside can be mutually
inconsistent even when the underlying thing is perfectly coherent.

* Where This Leads

At some point I stopped waiting for someone else to produce an answer I could believe and
started trying to construct one myself.

I want to be careful about what I'm claiming. I don't think I have solved anything. The
question of why there is something rather than nothing may not have a solution in any
conventional sense. What I have is a framework that is honest about its starting point ---
one that takes the question seriously instead of bracketing it --- and that makes contact
with actual physics in ways that can be checked.

What follows in Documents 1 through 5 is that attempt made mathematical. The open
problems are real and I haven't hidden them. But the math came after the question. This
document is the question.
